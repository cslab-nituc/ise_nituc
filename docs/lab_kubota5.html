Personal Information

Ryosuke KUBOTA was born in 1977. He received the B.E., the M.E. and the Ph.D. degrees from Kyushu Institute of Technology (KIT), Japan in 2000, 2002 and 2006, respectively. He was a research fellow of Knowledge Cluster Initiative, "Yamaguchi/Ube Medical Innovation Cluster" granted by Japan Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) at Yamaguchi University, Japan since 2006.

He is currently a professor of the Department of Intelligent Systems Engineering at Ube National College of Technology. His current research interest lies on neuro-computing, machine learning, evolutional computing, brain-like information processing, pattern classification, and their applications for medical image/signal processing.

He is a member of

  • IEICE,
  • The Japanese Society for Evolutionary Computation,
  • Japan Society for Fuzzy Theory and Intelligent Informatics (SOFT).
